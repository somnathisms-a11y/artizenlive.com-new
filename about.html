Our Origin Story

Born From a Frustrating Booking Experience

ArtizenLive was founded in 2021 in Kolkata after our founders experienced first-hand the chaos of unverified artist bookings — undisclosed cancellations, direct cash advances with zero accountability, and no recourse for quality issues.

We built ArtizenLive to solve this systemic problem: a tech-first marketplace where every performer is auditioned, background-checked, and technically verified — and where every rupee is protected through a neutral escrow engine until the performance is confirmed complete.

Today, we serve 10,000+ happy event hosts across 25+ Indian cities, from intimate sangeet functions to headline corporate galas.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every decision at ArtizenLive is guided by these principles — for our artists, for our clients, and for the live events ecosystem.

🛡️

Payment Safety First

Every booking is secured by our neutral escrow engine. No artist receives payment unless the event concludes successfully — protecting both parties at all times.

✅

Radical Transparency

We display clear artist profiles, verified client reviews, sound rider specifications, and pricing breakdowns — no hidden markups or last-minute surprises.

🎯

Artist Excellence Standards

Every artist on ArtizenLive passes a rigorous live audition, background verification, and document check before receiving a Verified Talent badge.

💬

Direct Artist Communication

After booking confirmation, clients communicate directly with the artist to customize setlists, timings, stage tech specs, and entry choreography.

⚡

24/7 Concierge Support

Our dedicated event concierge team is available around the clock for coordination, emergency replacements, and technical issue resolution.

🌱

Empowering Independent Artists

We champion India's independent performing talent by providing fair compensation, growing their online visibility, and enabling them to build sustainable careers.
