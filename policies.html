Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 1, 2026 · Effective Immediately

ArtizenLive is committed to protecting the privacy of all users — including event hosts, registered artists, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the controls available to you.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Name, email address, phone number, and password when creating an account.
  • Booking Data: Event date, venue address, performance type, and guest count submitted during booking flows.
  • Payment Data: Billing information is handled by our Payment Gateway Partner (Razorpay). ArtizenLive does not store full card numbers.
  • Usage Data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, and click-through data collected via cookies for analytics purposes.
  • Artist Media: Performance video clips, audio samples, and headshots uploaded by registered artists.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and manage booking requests between event hosts and artists.
  • To communicate booking confirmations, platform updates, and concierge support.
  • To improve platform performance, resolve technical issues, and personalize search results.
  • To comply with legal obligations under applicable Indian data protection laws.
🔒 Data Security: All user data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption in transit and at rest. Our servers are hosted on ISO-27001 certified infrastructure.

3. Information Sharing

ArtizenLive does not sell or rent user data to third parties. Your contact details are only shared with the booked artist after escrow lock confirmation — and only the minimum information required for event coordination.

4. Your Rights & Controls

  • Access & Download: Request a full export of your personal data at any time.
  • Account Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Marketing Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from email newsletters at any time via the link in any email.
  • Cookie Control: Disable non-essential cookies from your browser privacy settings.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use session cookies for user authentication and third-party analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to understand usage patterns. No tracking pixels are used for targeted advertising.

Privacy Inquiries

For data access requests, deletion, or general privacy concerns, contact our Data Protection Officer.

Escrow Payment Guarantee

Last Updated: July 1, 2026 · Effective Immediately

ArtizenLive's Escrow Guarantee is the cornerstone of our trust framework. It ensures that neither the event host nor the artist is ever financially vulnerable during the booking process.

🛡️ Core Promise: When you book an artist on ArtizenLive, your payment is held in a neutral third-party escrow account — and released to the artist only after your event concludes successfully.

How Escrow Works — Step by Step

  1. Host initiates booking and pays 100% of the performance fee via ArtizenLive's secure payment gateway.
  2. Funds are locked in an independent, audited escrow account managed by our banking partner.
  3. Artist receives confirmation and coordinates directly with the host for setlists, sound riders, and event logistics.
  4. Event Day: Artist performs. Host or their representative confirms completion on the ArtizenLive app or portal within 24 hours.
  5. Funds Released: Upon successful confirmation, escrow releases the artist's fee within 3–5 business days.

Dispute Resolution

  • Artist No-Show: If the artist fails to appear without approved prior notice, 100% of the escrow amount is immediately refunded to the host, and a complimentary replacement artist coordination is initiated.
  • Partial Performance: If the artist performs for less than 60% of the agreed duration, a proportional refund is issued from the escrow balance.
  • Quality Dispute: Sound quality, technical, or rider disputes must be raised within 48 hours of the event via support@artizenlive.com with video evidence.

Escrow Applicable Events

The escrow mechanism applies to all bookings made through ArtizenLive's platform. Offline bookings or direct payments made outside the platform are NOT covered by our Escrow Guarantee.

Escrow Disputes

Contact our Escrow Support Team for any booking payment issues or disputes.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: July 1, 2026 · Effective Immediately

We understand that event plans can change unexpectedly. Our Refund & Cancellation Policy is designed to be fair to both event hosts and performing artists while protecting their respective investments.

Host Cancellation Schedule

The following refund schedule applies when the event host initiates a cancellation:

Cancellation Timeline Refund to Host Artist Retention Status
More than 14 Days Before Event 100% Full Refund 0% Full Refund
7 to 14 Days Before Event 80% Refund 20% (Calendar Reservation) Partial Refund
48 Hours to 7 Days Before 50% Refund 50% (Preparation Costs) Partial Refund
Less than 48 Hours Before 20% Refund 80% Minimal Refund
On the Day of Event 0% Refund 100% No Refund

Artist Cancellation

If the artist cancels, the following guaranteed protections apply to the host:

  • 100% full refund of the escrow amount, processed within 2–3 business days.
  • Complimentary emergency replacement — our concierge team will actively source and connect you with an equivalent verified artist at no additional service charge.
  • The cancelling artist receives a trust score penalty and may be suspended from the platform.

Force Majeure

In the case of natural disasters, government-mandated lockdowns, or other events beyond reasonable control, both parties may agree to reschedule without financial penalty. ArtizenLive Concierge will mediate all force majeure cases.

Cancellation Requests

All cancellations must be submitted in writing through your ArtizenLive dashboard or by contacting our support team directly.

support@artizenlive.com
